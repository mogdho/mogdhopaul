What $name does

Watching a video and need to check something in another tab? The video follows you. It floats in a small window in the corner, so you can keep watching while you do something else.

  • WhenThe video
  • You switch to another tabThe video: Pops out
  • You switch to another appThe video: Pops out
  • You come back to YouTubeThe video: Goes back in

It only pops out a video that is playing. A paused video stays where it is. There are no settings and no buttons: add it and it just works.
