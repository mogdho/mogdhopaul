    How it’s worked out

    The rate
    Today’s exchange rate is filled in for you. If your bank or app shows a different one, type it in instead and everything updates.
    The 2.5% incentive
    Bangladesh adds 2.5% on top of money sent home through a bank or a licensed service like Remitly. So 10,000 TK converted becomes 10,250 TK when it arrives.
    Working backwards
    Choose How much should arrive to see what you need to send for a set amount in taka, incentive included.
