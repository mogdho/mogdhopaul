Source: confirmed with the owner in the 2026-10-27 brainstorm; full record in docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-site-redesign-design.md.

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a little vanilla JS, on GitHub Pages (custom domain mogdhopaul.com). A nightly GitHub Action (Python) pulls Instagram photos into the repo.

## Users

1. Search engines and people who type "Mogdho Paul" into Google and want to know who he is.
2. Secondary: people considering hiring him for web tools, SEO or video editing.

## Product Purpose

Be the top result for "Mogdho Paul" and the one place that clearly says who he is, linking all his profiles. Success: ranks #1 for the name; visitors leave knowing who he is and how to reach him.

## Positioning

The personal home of one specific person: Mogdho Paul, a developer and SEO specialist from Bangladesh who builds small web tools and automations, also edits video, and travels and photographs. Only he has these photos, this story and these tools.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: Home, About, Photos, Projects. English only.
- Identity line (verbatim everywhere): "Mogdho Paul is a developer and SEO specialist from Bangladesh who builds small web tools and automations."
- Only spelling "Mogdho Paul". Location Bangladesh. Video editing appears but not as headline role.
- All key text and photos present in HTML without JS. Google Analytics G-LDY4Z5D7MW stays.
- Photos show only place and date, never full captions.

## Brand Commitments

- Owner approved visual direction: "a quiet travel notebook": calm, personal, paper and ink, one earthy accent, photos like album prints, his name in Morse code, almost no motion.
- Voice: his own plain, honest, slightly shy first person (introvert who loves learning). No marketing phrases.

## Evidence on Hand

- Profile photo: mogdhopaul.jpeg. Instagram travel photos (via nightly feed). GitHub repos at github.com/mogdho. Personal story at maggy2minutes.wordpress.com/about/. Video showcase at video-showcase.mogdhopaul.com.
- No testimonials, client lists or metrics may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Plain text Google can read beats effects.
2. One consistent identity everywhere.
3. Made by a person, not a template.
4. Quiet over loud.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus visible, reduced motion respected, readable on low-end phones on mobile data.
