yt-mini-PIP

Your YouTube video keeps playing in a small floating window when you switch tabs, and slides back when you return.

Download yt-mini-PIP

Free · Chrome, Edge and Brave · No account

What yt-mini-PIP does

Watching a video and need to check something in another tab? The video follows you. It floats in a small window in the corner, so you can keep watching while you do something else.

  • WhenThe video
  • You switch to another tabThe video: Pops out
  • You switch to another appThe video: Pops out
  • You come back to YouTubeThe video: Goes back in

It only pops out a video that is playing. A paused video stays where it is. There are no settings and no buttons: add it and it just works.

Add it to Chrome in two minutes

It isn’t in the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it yourself. It only takes a few clicks.

  1. Download it

    Click the download button. You get one file, yt-mini-pip-v1.1.zip.

  2. Unzip it

    Right-click the file, choose Extract All, then Extract. You get a folder called yt-mini-pip-v1.1.

    Keep this folder. Move it somewhere it can stay, like your Documents folder. Chrome uses it every time it opens, so deleting it removes yt-mini-PIP.

  3. Open Chrome’s extensions page

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. Switch on Developer mode at the top right.

    Developer mode only lets you add extensions from a folder on your computer. Nothing else changes.

  4. Add yt-mini-PIP

    Click Load unpacked and choose the yt-mini-pip-v1.1 folder. yt-mini-PIP appears in the list, switched on and ready.

Using Edge or Brave? It works there too: type edge://extensions or brave://extensions instead. In Edge, Developer mode is in the menu on the left.

Want more?

ytModify does the same pop-out, and adds a soft glow around the video, the video quality you want and a bigger player. Use one or the other, not both.

Updating or removing it

Get a new version
Download it again and unzip it into the same yt-mini-pip-v1.1 folder, replacing the old files. Then open chrome://extensions and click the round reload arrow on yt-mini-PIP.
Remove it
Open chrome://extensions, click Remove on yt-mini-PIP, then delete the yt-mini-pip-v1.1 folder.
