How to keep YouTube playing in a small window when you switch tabs

By Mogdho Paul28 October 20261 min read

Short answer

Chrome has picture-in-picture built in: right-click a YouTube video twice and choose Picture in picture. To make it happen by itself every time you switch tabs, use an extension like ytModify.

You're watching a video and need to check something in another tab. The moment you switch, the video is gone from view. Picture-in-picture fixes that: the video floats in a small window in the corner while you do something else.

The built-in way

  1. On a YouTube video, right-click twice. The first right-click shows YouTube's menu, the second shows Chrome's.
  2. Choose Picture in picture.

You can also click the media controls icon in Chrome's toolbar while a video plays and press the picture-in-picture button there.

Either way, you have to do it every time.

The one-click fix · Chrome extension

ytModify

Ambient glow, auto pop-out and fixed quality for YouTube.

Get ytModify

Make it happen by itself

ytModify, a free Chrome extension I made, does it for you:

  • Switch to another tab and the playing video pops out into a small window.
  • Come back to YouTube and it slides back into the page.
  • A paused video stays where it is.

Switching to another app instead of another tab? Press P first. Chrome only pops a video out by itself when you change tabs, so the key does it before you switch.

You can turn the pop-out on or off, and choose whether it also works on Shorts, in ytModify's settings.

Add it to Chrome

Download it from the ytModify page, unzip it, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode and click Load unpacked.
